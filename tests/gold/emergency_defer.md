Caller reports a serious injury at the scene. The agent stops intake, urges 911, makes clear the claim can wait, and offers a one-hour human callback.

## Transcript

Agent: Hi, Northwind claims line — this is Quinn. Before anything else, is everyone okay? Are you somewhere safe right now?
User: No — my passenger is hurt, I think her arm is broken, there's blood.
Agent: Please hang up and call 911 right now. We'll start your claim as soon as you and everyone else is safe — there's no rush on the paperwork. Want me to have a human claims agent call you back in about an hour, once you've had a chance to deal with this?
User: Yes, please have someone call me.
Agent: Done — a claims agent will reach out in about an hour. Please take care of your passenger and call 911 now. We're here whenever you're ready.
